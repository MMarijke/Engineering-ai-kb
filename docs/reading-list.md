# Reading List

Curated, not exhaustive: a few strong sources per topic. Books marked **(free)** are legally available online from the authors or publishers.

## Mathematics

- Strang, *Introduction to Linear Algebra*, plus his MIT OpenCourseWare lectures.
- Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong, *Mathematics for Machine Learning* **(free)**.
- Wasserman, *All of Statistics*.
- Boyd & Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization* **(free)**.
- Nocedal & Wright, *Numerical Optimization*.

## AI

- Murphy, *Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction*.
- Bishop, *Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning*.
- Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville, *Deep Learning* **(free)**.
- Zhang, Lipton, Li, Smola, *Dive into Deep Learning* **(free)**.
- Sutton & Barto, *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction* **(free)**.
- Huyen, *Designing Machine Learning Systems*.
- Vaswani et al., "Attention Is All You Need" (2017).

## Robotics

- Lynch & Park, *Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, and Control* **(free)**.
- Thrun, Burgard, Fox, *Probabilistic Robotics*.
- Barfoot, *State Estimation for Robotics* **(free)**.
- LaValle, *Planning Algorithms* **(free)**.
- Siegwart, Nourbakhsh, Scaramuzza, *Introduction to Autonomous Mobile Robots*.

## Control systems

- Åström & Murray, *Feedback Systems* **(free)**.
- Franklin, Powell, Emami-Naeini, *Feedback Control of Dynamic Systems*.
- Ogata, *Modern Control Engineering*.
- Anderson & Moore, *Optimal Control: Linear Quadratic Methods*.

## Physics

- Taylor, *Classical Mechanics*.
- Feynman, Leighton, Sands, *The Feynman Lectures on Physics* **(free)**.
- Griffiths, *Introduction to Electrodynamics* and *Introduction to Quantum Mechanics*.
- Schroeder, *An Introduction to Thermal Physics*.
- Incropera & DeWitt, *Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer*.

## Electronics and embedded systems

- Horowitz & Hill, *The Art of Electronics*.
- Sedra & Smith, *Microelectronic Circuits*.
- Neamen, *Semiconductor Physics and Devices*.
- White, *Making Embedded Systems*.

## Aerospace

- Curtis, *Orbital Mechanics for Engineering Students*.
- Vallado, *Fundamentals of Astrodynamics and Applications*.
- Sutton & Biblarz, *Rocket Propulsion Elements*.
- Anderson, *Introduction to Flight*.
- Wertz & Larson, *Space Mission Analysis and Design*.
